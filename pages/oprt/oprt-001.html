Mechanical Recycling Line
(No Canary GmbH)

The Battery-to-Black Mass Process enables mechanical battery recycling from battery modules to multiple particle fractions. The batteries are crushed in a vacuum and a raw material concentrate called Black Mass as well as other fractions like the low-boiling organic solvents of the electrolyte, heavy parts, metal foils, and plastic foils are recovered.

From Battery Module to Material Fractions

The process is divided into two modules. The pre-module opens the battery and separates the low-boiling organic solvents of the electrolyte from the shredded material. The product module separates the dried shredded material from the batteries into different fractions for further processing

Crushing and Drying

After all necessary ambient conditions (pressure, temperature) are ready the battery module is crushed. Directly after the opening of the cell casings the low-boiling organic solvents of the electrolyte start to condensate at the vaccuum pump system. The crushed material gets dried in a heatable high-intensity mixer.

Particle Separation

The dried materials are put through multiple steps of physical separation based on particle density/shape and particle size. The aim is to achieve a heavy fraction (consisting mainly of iron, copper, aluminum and plastic), a Black Mass fraction (a raw material concentrate consisting of lithium, cobalt, nickel, graphite and manganese), a metal foil fraction (aluminum, copper) and a plastic foil fraction
